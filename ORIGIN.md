# Origin and authorship boundary

This repository is a public portfolio artifact authored by Dmitry Nesmachny.

The case is derived from professional architecture-review experience involving a distributed enterprise platform. The original work included review of a corporate conceptual architecture and recommendations for making distributed-node governance, lifecycle management, autonomy, observability and operational responsibility explicit.

The original corporate documents are **not published** in this repository.

The public case was reconstructed as a new synthetic architecture scenario. It intentionally removes or replaces:

- employer, customer and organizational names;
- project/program/system identifiers;
- corporate terminology and internal standards;
- real infrastructure and security-zone details;
- implementation-specific product names where they are not required to explain the pattern;
- participant names and correspondence;
- original diagrams and document excerpts.

The repository demonstrates the author's architecture reasoning: identifying an operational governance gap in a federated design, separating control and execution concerns, defining node profiles and lifecycle, and establishing explicit resilience and synchronization semantics.
